We are pleased to submit our regular paper manuscript entitled \textbf{``Complex Wasserstein State-Space Network: Geodesic-Modulated Rotational Dynamics for Unified Visual Restoration and Foreground Pattern Analysis''} for consideration for publication in \emph{IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)}. Rather than proposing a task-specific image processing pipeline suited for \emph{IEEE TIP}, this work introduces a foundational, mathematically rigorous visual representation operator---\textbf{Complex Wasserstein State-Space Operator (CW-SSO)}---backed by two white-box theoretical guarantees (Theorems 1 and 2) and validated across both unified multi-degradation visual restoration (3D/5D/10D All-in-One benchmarks) and four Universal Foreground Pattern Analysis tasks (Camouflaged Object, Defocus Blur, Image Forgery, and Shadow Detection).

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Summary of Core Technical & Theoretical Contributions:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Closed-Form Wasserstein Geodesic Discretization:} We reformulate the continuous-to-discrete transition step size $\tilde{\Delta}_t$ of selective state-space differential equations using the exact closed-form 2-Wasserstein distance $\mathcal{W}_2^2(\mathcal{P}_t, \mathcal{P}_{t-1})$ between local Gaussian neighborhoods, establishing an exponential upper bound on cross-region state contamination (Theorem 2).
    \item \textbf{$2 \times 2$ Orthogonal Rotational Spectral State-Space:} We lift the state evolution matrix to the complex domain $A = -\Lambda + i\Omega$ and execute its discretized recurrence via an isomorphic real-valued $2 \times 2$ block-orthogonal parallel prefix scan in $O(\log_2 L)$ steps natively in FP16, conserving high-frequency phase energy (Theorem 1) without external FFT modules.
    \item \textbf{Extreme Parameter Efficiency & Broad Pattern Analysis Generalization:} Stacking CW-SSO homogeneously yields \textbf{CW-SSN-T} (5.81M parameters, 88.4\% lighter than DA-RCOT~\emph{TPAMI 2025} at 50.1M) and \textbf{CWSSOAdapter} (0.99M parameters, only 1.61\% overhead on frozen SegFormer-B4 vs.\ EVP's 5.70\% on \emph{TPAMI 2025}), achieving state-of-the-art performance across 10 restoration degradations and 4 foreground segmentation benchmarks.
\end{enumerate}
